# Question categories

Pick the closest category and use its sub-questions as a starting point. Drop what does not apply.

## Product or tool

- What is it, who makes it, and what is it for?
- Current version, release date, price and license.
- Main strengths and known problems, from users and independent reviews.
- Who it is a good fit for, and who it is not.

## Comparison

- Which options exist, and which are realistic for the user's case?
- The same attributes for each option (price, features, limits, maturity), in a table.
- Where independent sources disagree about which is better.
- A recommendation for the user's stated case, and what would change it.

## How-to

- What is the goal and what are the prerequisites?
- The steps, in order, from official documentation first.
- Common mistakes and how to check that it worked.
- Alternatives when the main route does not fit.

## Fact check or landscape

- What exactly is the claim, and where does it come from?
- The best primary evidence for and against, with dates.
- What experts or official bodies say, and how they differ.
- A verdict with a confidence level: supported, partly supported, unsupported, or unclear.
